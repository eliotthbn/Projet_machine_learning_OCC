# Week 1 : Project Definition and Data Collection
• Form teams of 2–4 students. <br>
• Select a topic related to your major. <br>
• Define the problem and research question. <br>
• Identify and collect a suitable dataset. <br>
• Perform an initial exploratory data analysis.<br>
Deliverable: Project specification document (2–3 pages), including the problem statement, dataset description, objectives and initial work plan.
